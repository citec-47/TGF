About Transgenerational Forces

About
TGF Group.

Transgenerational Forces (TGF) is a forward-looking group of companies committed to creating solutions that improve everyday life and make a lasting difference across generations. Founded on resilience, innovation and a passion for transformation, TGF has grown from humble beginnings into a dynamic group with diverse operations. Since its founding on 17 June 2018, TGF has remained steadfast in its mission to empower people, shape industries and leave an enduring legacy. Guided by its watchword, “Base For Better Living,” the Group develops initiatives and enterprises that create value today and inspire future generations. Through its subsidiaries and Corporate Social Responsibility initiatives, TGF recognises, empowers and supports young people as they pursue their ambitions.

Our vision

Create value today while inspiring generations to come.

Our mission

Empower people, shape industries, and leave an enduring legacy.

What guides our work

    Our core values

    Five principles define our culture and standards.

    A Transgenerationer is someone who lives by these values.

    1. 01
      Service

      We believe leadership is defined not by title but by service. Inspired by Matthew 23:11–12—“The greatest among you shall be your servant”—we lead with humility and a willingness to work for the good of others.

    2. 02
      Giving

      Giving is a way of life and an expression of love and commitment to our vision. We contribute our time, resources and effort to advance the Group’s mission and help others succeed.

    3. 03
      Loyalty

      Loyalty is demonstrated through unity and commitment to upholding the dignity and integrity of the Group. We value every member and treat each person with respect, regardless of age, background or race.

    4. 04
      Discipline & Punctuality

      Excellence requires discipline. We treat punctuality as a mark of respect, professionalism and integrity, and hold ourselves accountable for consistent, dependable work.

    5. 05
      Resilience

      Resilience keeps the vision moving forward. We persevere through difficulty, adapt to change and see our work through to completion.

    TGFEA / 01

    Transgenerational Forces
    Empowerment & Awards

    ✳

    Transgenerational Forces Empowerment & Awards (TGFEA) is a flagship annual programme by TGF Group, designed to empower, connect and recognise visionaries, entrepreneurs, nation builders and other impact-driven individuals across Africa and beyond.

    Its two-day experience combines empowerment, networking and global recognition. Day one focuses on networking, collaboration and growth; day two is an awards and recognition ceremony.

    Published schedule: Held annually on the third Saturday and Sunday of January.

        TGFVP / 02

        Transgenerational Forces
        Vocational Pathways

        ↗

        TGF Vocational Pathways offers certified vocational programmes designed to equip participants with practical skills and industry-relevant knowledge through hands-on learning.

        Programme length

        12 months. Each academic cycle begins on the first Monday of November. Programmes are designed to be completed on or before September 30 of the following year.

        Graduation

        Graduation ceremonies are scheduled for the fourth Saturday of October each year.

        Learning options

        TGF training centres in Douala and Yaounde, with online participation depending on participant needs and availability.

        Class schedule

        Classes are generally held twice weekly, with sessions of approximately two hours. Schedules may be adjusted for programme or student needs.

        After successful completion

        Successful participants receive a TGF Group Certificate of Completion.
